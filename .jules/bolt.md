## 2024-10-07 - Base-2 Math in ESP32-P4 DSP Loops
**Learning:** In hot audio DSP loops on the ESP32-P4, using base-10 functions (e.g., `std::pow(10.0f, ...)`, `std::log10`) can be significantly slower. Base-2 functions (`std::exp2`, `std::log2`) combined with precomputed constant multipliers for the conversion (e.g., `* 0.1660964f` instead of `/ 20`) provide a massive performance gain (e.g. 70us to ~58us per block). However, exact-equivalence unit tests will fail and require a small floating-point tolerance (e.g., `1e-4f`) to accommodate these optimizations.
**Action:** When working on DSP hot paths, actively look for base-10 math operations and convert them to base-2 with precomputed multipliers. Update any bit-exact tests to use a reasonable floating-point tolerance when making this change.
