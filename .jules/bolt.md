## 2024-09-20 - Fast Pow10/Log10 approximations with exp2/log2
**Learning:** `std::pow(10.0f, x / 20)` and `std::log10(x)` are heavily used in audio dynamics processing (compressors, limiters, gates) and are computationally expensive. These can be translated into `std::exp2` and `std::log2` with precomputed constants. Since ESP32-P4 and generic DSPs often have faster intrinsics or better approximations for base-2 transcedentals compared to base-10, this provides a measurable performance win. When checking equivalence, exact bitwise equality will fail since the math is an approximation, so a small floating-point tolerance (e.g. `1e-6f`) must be added to exact-equivalence unit tests.
**Action:** When working in hot audio loops, replace `std::pow(10, x)` and `std::log10(x)` with `std::exp2(x * log2(10))` and `std::log2(x) / log2(10)` combined with folded constants to save CPU cycles.
